# GoSource Design Rules

> Scope: customer web app, admin web app, and shared UI system

These rules keep the product visually calm, professional, and consistent.

## Design Principles

- Clarity over decoration.
- Consistency over novelty.
- Usability over visual experimentation.
- Structure over flourish.

## Visual Direction

- Use one brand color and a restrained neutral palette.
- Prefer borders over heavy shadows.
- Keep layouts clean and readable.
- Avoid decorative gradients and flashy effects.
- Keep the product feeling operational, not marketing-heavy.

## Typography Rules

- Use a small, disciplined typographic scale.
- Keep heading hierarchy obvious.
- Use medium or semibold weights where possible.
- Avoid oversized headings and excessive font variety.

## Spacing Rules

- Use a consistent spacing scale.
- Keep vertical rhythm predictable.
- Avoid arbitrary spacing values.
- Do not make pages feel cramped just to fit more content.

## Component Rules

- Use `shadcn-vue`-style components as the base.
- Buttons, inputs, cards, and similar controls should feel like one system.
- Reuse approved component patterns before inventing new ones.
- Do not restyle components ad hoc on each page.

## Motion Rules

- Motion should be functional only.
- Keep transitions subtle and short.
- Avoid bouncing, parallax, and decorative animation.
- Do not use motion as the main hierarchy signal.

## Layout Rules

- Keep primary actions predictable.
- Avoid unnecessary nesting.
- Use clear alignment and breathing room.
- Keep dashboards dense but not cramped.

## Accessibility Rules

- Maintain readable contrast.
- Keep focus states visible.
- Ensure interaction targets are large enough.
- Preserve keyboard usability.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use random accent colors.
- Do not use decorative shadows or glassmorphism.
- Do not build “pretty” layouts that reduce clarity.
- Do not let one-off page styling fight the system.
